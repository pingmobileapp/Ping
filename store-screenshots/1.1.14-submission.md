## What's New in This Version

Made for iPhone Duo and iPad
• Open your iPhone Duo and your calendar sits on one side with Upcoming on the other.
• Slide Upcoming away to see the full week, then slide it back with a tap.
• Works the same on iPad, and Ping fits neatly on the Duo's cover screen.

Also
• The week title now shows exactly the days on screen.

## App Review Notes (paste after the 1.1.13 notes, or replace the "What's new" part)

What's new in 1.1.14:
1. iPhone Duo support (built with the iOS 27.1 SDK). Unfolded, Home shows the calendar on the left and Upcoming on the right, split at the fold. A handle on the divider slides Upcoming away for a full-width calendar. Folded, the app uses the cover screen and stays clear of the side controls.
2. iPad uses the same two-pane Home.
