Ping — Support

Questions, feedback, or something not working right? Reach out any time at hello@hivebuilt.dev and we'll get back to you.

How do invites work?

When you invite someone, Ping checks whether they already have an account. If they do, they get notified right in the app. If they don't, you can text them the invite straight from your own Messages app — no extra setup on their end, and it comes from your number, not a business number.

How do I RSVP?

Open the event and choose Accept, Interested, or Decline. You can change your response any time — the host and the guest list update automatically.

How do I leave a group or stop notifications for an event?

Open the event or group's chat and use the mute option to stop notifications without leaving. To be removed from a group entirely, ask the group's owner, or contact us and we can help.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open your profile menu, then Settings → Delete Account. It deletes your account along with your events, messages, and photos. If you can't sign in, email hello@hivebuilt.dev from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.
