Connect it

Meta Muse

Ping will show up in Muse's connector list once Meta finishes reviewing it. Then open Muse, find Ping in its connectors, and sign in.

Claude

  1. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Ping and paste the connector address above.
  3. Choose Connect. A Ping page opens. Sign in the way you do in the app.
  4. Pick Full access or Read only, then Allow.

ChatGPT and other AI apps

Any AI app that can add a remote MCP connector works the same way: add a connector, paste the address above, then sign in to Ping and allow it. In ChatGPT this is under Settings → Apps & Connectors (developer mode).

Signing in

  • Google or email and password: use them just like in the app.
  • Signed up with Apple: choose Email me a sign-in link and enter the email on your Apple ID. If you chose “Hide My Email”, use that relay address. The link brings you back to finish connecting.

Full access or Read only

Read only lets the AI app look at your events, groups, people and Discover, but not change anything.

Full access also lets it make and edit events, Invite People, RSVP, add to What to bring, post in event chats and star things in Discover. Anything that reaches other people (invites, RSVPs, chat messages, canceling an event) is marked so your AI app asks you first.

An AI app can never delete your Ping account or pay for anything. Do those in the Ping app.

What it can't see

Personal items saved only in your phone's own calendar stay on your phone. The AI app sees your Ping events and the Discover activities you starred. People without the Ping app are added to your guest list, but their invite goes out by text from the Ping app on your phone.

Disconnect

In the Ping app, open Settings → Connected AI and tap Disconnect next to the AI app. It loses access right away. You can also remove the connector in the AI app.
